# Taste: "Official document"

The site should feel like a well-made legal document: calm, exact, trustworthy. Not a startup landing page, not a newspaper.

## Direction
- Paper and ink. Cool off-white paper `#FAFAF7`, ink navy `#1B2B48`, soft ink `#4A5874`, rules `#D9DCE3`.
- Brass `#9C7A2B` appears only on the seal logo. Stamp red `#8E2B2B` only on the not-an-attorney disclaimer.
- Type: Libre Caslon Text for headings (legal-document heritage), Public Sans for body (civic, plain). Sentence case everywhere.
- One memorable element: the embossed-seal text logo. Keep everything else quiet.
- Fees read like a printed fee schedule (dotted leaders between service and price), never pricing cards.
- Left-aligned single column, line length under 70ch.

## Avoid
- Identical rounded cards with shadows, gradient washes, all-caps eyebrow labels, middle-dot meta strings, arrows on buttons.
- Cream + terracotta, black + neon, broadsheet hairline columns.
- Anything that could look like a government seal or imply state affiliation.

## Quality floor
Mobile first (16px gutters, no sideways scroll), visible keyboard focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, WCAG AA contrast.
