Questions

What ID should I bring?

A current, unexpired government photo ID: driver's license, non-driver state ID, passport, or military ID. The name should match the name on your document. Each signer needs their own ID.

Should I sign before we meet?

No. Bring the document unsigned. You'll sign in front of me after I check your ID.

What can't a notary do?
  • Give legal advice or tell you which form you need
  • Prepare, draft, or fill in legal documents for you
  • Notarize for a signer who isn't present (in person, or on a lawful video call for electronic notarization)
  • Notarize a document with blank spaces
  • Notarize for someone who seems not to understand the document or is being pressured
  • Certify copies of documents, including birth, death, or marriage certificates
  • Notarize a document I have a personal or financial interest in
  • Issue an apostille (only the NYS Department of State can)
How much will it cost?

$2 per signature, as set by New York law. Electronic notarizations are up to $25. If I travel to you, the travel fee is quoted before we book.

Can you help with my will or immigration papers?

I can notarize signatures on documents you've already prepared, but I can't advise you on them. For legal questions, talk to a licensed New York attorney.
