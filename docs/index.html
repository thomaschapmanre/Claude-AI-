FAQ

What ID do I need to bring?

A current, unexpired government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license, non-driver state ID, U.S. or foreign passport, or military ID. The name on your ID should match the name on the document. Every person signing needs their own ID.

Should I sign my document before we meet?

No. Bring it unsigned. You'll sign in front of me after I verify your identity.

What can't a notary do?
  • Give legal advice or tell you which form you need
  • Prepare, draft, or fill in legal documents for you
  • Notarize a signature if the signer isn't physically present (or, for electronic notarization, on a lawful audio-video call)
  • Notarize a document with blank spaces
  • Notarize for someone who appears not to understand the document or is being pressured to sign
  • Certify copies of documents, including birth, death, or marriage certificates
  • Notarize a document in which I have a personal or financial interest
  • Issue an apostille (only the NYS Department of State can)
How much will it cost?

$2 per signature for in-person notarizations, as set by NYS law. Electronic notarizations are up to $25. If I travel to you, there's a separate travel fee that I quote before the appointment.

Can you help with my will, immigration papers, or other legal matter?

I can notarize signatures on documents you've already prepared, but I can't advise you on them. For legal questions, please talk to a licensed New York attorney.
