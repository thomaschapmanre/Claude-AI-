Delq Packets

Drop in the month's MRI late letters report and pick who gets a packet. Tenants who owe less than the small balance limit get a small balance letter. Everyone else gets a 14-day POQ, Good Cause notice and Lease Violation notice. Each tenant and co-occupant gets their own copy.

3 · Tenants and breakdowns

Tick each tenant getting a packet. Anyone who owes less than the small balance limit is set to a small balance letter; you can switch any tenant in their breakdown. Every resident listed under a tenant, co-occupants included, gets their own documents. Small balance letters show the report balance as it is, with no added fees. Late fee (LAT) and damage (DAM) lines from the report are left off the POQ; the late and damage fees above are added as their own lines, which you can change or remove.

4 · Review and download

Your report is read in your browser; nothing is uploaded. The print packet is one PDF in print order, unit by unit: a small balance letter for each resident under the limit, and for everyone else the 14-day POQ, then the Good Cause notice, then the Lease Violation notice. If you need to change wording first, the Word download has the same documents as editable files, one folder per unit. Small balance letters come as PDFs in that download. Lease start dates and co-occupants you add are remembered on this device for next month.

3 · Who was served

Only for eviction filings, after the notices are served. Uses your attorney's Midtown affidavit of service. Every resident on an eviction packet is listed by unit, co-occupants under the main resident, and each gets their own affidavit. Add a co-occupant here if they're not on the report; they're added to that unit's notice packet too. Names and units come from the report; fill in how and when each person was served. The affidavit covers the 14-day POQ, as the form is written. Mailing (first-class and certified) and the military statement are marked on every affidavit.
